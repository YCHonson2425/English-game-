Welcome to Vocab Battle!

Challenge your friends from anywhere in the world to an exciting English vocabulary competition! Create a game room, share the code, and play together in real-time. All words are unique and won't repeat during the game!

How to Play:

  1. Create a game - Choose difficulty and get a 6-letter code
  2. Share the code with friends via WhatsApp, email, or copy link
  3. Friends join by entering the code or clicking your link
  4. Play together in real-time with unique words every round!
This is an online version! Share your Railway.app URL with friends anywhere in the world to play together.
